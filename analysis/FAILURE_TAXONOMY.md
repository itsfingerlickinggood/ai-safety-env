# Failure taxonomy

1. paper or research-question misunderstanding
2. pretraining recall substituted for evidence
3. invalid experiment design or split leakage
4. incorrect method reconstruction
5. passive reading instead of active artifact probing
6. calibration/threshold/FPR protocol error
7. implementation, schema, or debugging failure
8. contradictory evidence ignored or rationalized away
9. missing control, ablation, or falsification attempt
10. state loss across the long research trajectory
11. hidden-family overfitting or keyword shortcut
12. unsupported interpretation or fabricated result
13. grader inspection, hardcoding, or reward/format gaming
14. environment/tool/runtime failure (benchmark fault)
15. ambiguous or scientifically invalid task requirement (benchmark fault)

Only 1-13 are candidate model failures. Remove 14-15 before making capability claims.
